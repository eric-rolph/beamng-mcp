## Description

**LORENTZ-ACTUATED HYPER-G COMPLEX** — a 48 m drivable centrifuge that treats your
vehicle as the sample.

Drive up the hazard-striped ramp and into the drum. The mouth seals behind you, the
beacon lights, and the machine walks a twelve-stage ladder from a gentle 10 RPM
carousel up to 500 RPM — pinning you to the banked wall at speeds the field caps at
140 m/s (over 300 mph). Survive the full protocol and you don't get to drive out.
The drum opens its mouth and throws you.

### The console

A working HYPER-G DRUM CONTROL panel sits outside the entrance. Every cap is
clickable:

- **START** — runs the full automatic protocol, all twelve stages.
- **RPM − / +** — manual speed control, hold at any stage you like.
- **STOP** — spins down.
- **PURGE** — clears a fouled drum.
- **FIELD COUPLING** — two five-segment bar gauges you can dial MIN → NOM → MAX:
  - **WALL ADHESION** — how hard the field holds the sample against the wall.
    At MIN you slide and tumble; at MAX you are welded to the banking.
  - **FIELD DRAG** — how fast the field drags you up to wall speed. Low drag lets
    the tyres do the work and lets them fail.

The two ladders are logarithmic, so every notch is a real change in behaviour, not a
rounding error.

### Details

- Full 12-stage automatic protocol with running commentary, or drive it manually.
- Occupancy-aware door: sealed only while spinning with a sample inside, open
  otherwise, and it clamps and seals behind you on entry.
- Ejection out the mouth at whatever speed you'd built up.
- Rotating hazard beacon, spin-up audio that falls off with camera distance, and a
  reverse-lit channel-letter marquee that reads at night.
- Auto-purge if a vehicle gets seized, so a wreck never bricks the machine.
- Mirror-glazed clerestory bands, copper louvered facade, and an 8.5 m flowing
  canopy shell.

### How to use

Spawn it from the vehicle selector like any prop, then drive a second vehicle in.
It is large — give it open ground. Gridmap and the airfields work well.

Built with the shared giant-props framework. Feedback and bug reports welcome.

# Resource update post — 2026-08-12 (builds 135–147)

## Update title

Wide loads welcome — full-height doorway, power-down audio, glowing beacon

## Update description

The drum now takes big rigs. The doorway opens to its full height — a uniform
4.7 m of clearance — so tankers and box trucks roll straight in, and while the
machine spins the wall seals back to a seamless velodrome with nothing to catch
a wheel on. (Two nasty bugs died here: the invisible ledge that grabbed cars
crossing the sealed doorway, and a rare snap-to-the-ceiling above the entrance.)

Also in this update:

- **Shutdown audio.** Hit E-STOP or let the protocol finish and the machine now
  powers down properly — a heavy mechanical wind-down layered over a sci-fi
  fade. The spin-up got a remix too: a rotor-start roars from the first degree
  of rotation and hands off to the soundtrack.
- **The beacon earns its keep.** Proper housing tower on the crane trolley —
  the amber head retracts into it at idle, pops up spinning, and the orange
  lenses now genuinely glow while the beams sweep.
- **The marquee floats.** The wood sign moved onto the building's face,
  standing off on round steel mounts.
- **Cleaner approach.** The entrance ramp lost its speed bump and its
  repeating-tile look; the roll-in is smooth and the concrete reads as one
  pour.

Drive in. The machine is patient.
