## Description

**THE GLASS ATRIUM** — a ten-storey steel-framed tower where every floor is a
12-metre pane of breakable glass, and your car is the drop test.

Drive onto the hazard-striped freight lift out front. "Freight lift engaged.
Going UP." Then the good part: a three-second countdown while the whole glass
stack you are about to fall through glitters above you. At zero the lift
slings you to 2.5 m above the open skylight and lets go — a 40 m fall to the
lobby — with a parting message: ENJOY THE VIEW. ALL TEN OF THEM.

Every floor you hit is real. The pane shatters, the sheet falls away, and the
steel frame arrests a slice of your fall speed at each level. An on-screen
ticker calls each floor as it breaks, with your live speed in mph, until you
flop onto the big blue crash cushion — TOUCHDOWN, a dust puff, and a scorecard
of floors shattered. Roll out through the archway and the glaziers are
dispatched — six seconds after touchdown, the moment the tower is clear, all
ten floors grow back for the next run.

### The glass

Not a scripted animation: every pane is a real jbeam sheet hung from the frame
on brittle hangers, and the whole surface lets go the instant its hangers
snap. The hanger ladder is tuned like a dare — the skylight floor pops at
28 kN, every storey below adds 6.5 kN, and the lobby floor holds 86.5 kN,
three times the top — calibrated in live testing under a 1.35-tonne ETK800 so
an ordinary car punches clean through all ten.

### The speed brake

A 40 m free fall through plate glass should not end with "drive out the
archway", so the tower brakes you as it breaks: every shatter clamps your fall
speed back to 6.5 m/s (about 23 km/h / 15 mph), and a final arrest three
metres above the cushion catches whatever the last floor missed. You get the
whole ten-floor demolition, and then you get to leave.

### Details

- Ten 12 m × 12 m breakable glass floors spaced 3.2 m apart under an open
  skylight rim 39 m up.
- Three-second on-screen countdown on the lift pad — the anticipation is the
  point.
- Brittle-hanger break ladder: 28 kN at the skylight, +6.5 kN per storey,
  86.5 kN at the lobby.
- Progressive speed brake: every shatter clamps the fall back to 6.5 m/s,
  plus a final arrest 3 m above the cushion.
- Per-floor shatter callouts with a live mph readout, and a touchdown
  scorecard counting the panes you took down.
- Landing dust on the quilted blue crash cushion; drive out through the
  5.2 m exit archway.
- Self-restoring: the glazier clock starts at touchdown, and once its six
  seconds have run and the lobby and shaft are empty, all ten floors
  regenerate. If a drop goes 30 seconds without a touchdown — say you stall
  on unbroken glass — the lift hands you back to the pad instead of
  stranding you.

### How to use

Spawn it from the vehicle selector like any prop, then drive a second vehicle
onto the hazard-striped lift pad at the front of the tower. Any overlap starts
the countdown; roll off mid-count and the lift stands down, and if the
glaziers are still sweeping it will ask you to hold. The footprint is just
under 14 m square and the tower stands about 40 m tall — give it flat, open
ground. Gridmap and the airfields work well.

Built with the shared giant-props framework. Feedback and bug reports welcome.

Enjoy the view. All ten of them.
