## Description

**200MPH SPIDER WEB CATCHER** — a giant orb web strung between two steel
towers at the end of a marked run-up, and your car is the fly.

Commit to the approach. Arrive carrying real speed — over 90 km/h (56 mph) —
and the web notices: "A FLY! The web glistens hungrily." Dawdle in slower and
it taunts you instead: the web prefers its flies FAST. 200 MPH, please. Then
you hit the silk. Nothing here is an animation — the net is free silk nodes
on soft elastic strands, each strand skinned with its own ribbon — so the
whole web balloons visibly metres deep, kicks up dust, and arrests a 200 mph
car over about 15 metres of travel. The hardest hits snap outer strands
outright. When you finally stop buzzing — under 36 km/h (22 mph) — the call
goes up: CAUGHT. Something is coming down to look at you.

### The silk

The web is real soft-body physics: 8 spokes, 5 rings, 15 m across, centred
8.5 m up between worn-steel towers 21 m apart and 16.5 m tall. Strands carry
finite break strength, grouped sector by sector, so a violent enough arrival
tears the outer web open right where you landed. The catch is honest, too:
you only count as caught after most of a second in the silk below 36 km/h.
Keep your speed up for 12 seconds — or blast clean through — and the web
writes you off as escaped.

### The spider

Once the fly stops struggling, the resident descends from the crossbeam on a
silk line: 6.2 metres in 2.6 seconds, swaying as it comes. It hangs over you,
bobbing, for a full five-second inspection — "You appear... crunchy." — then
delivers the verdict ("Not ripe yet.") and climbs back up in two seconds to
wait for the next fly. Black and red, yellow-eyed, endlessly patient.

### Details

- Full sequence in play order: inbound taunt, silk impact with a dust burst,
  elastic arrest, the CAUGHT call, spider descend / inspect / retreat, then
  a self re-spin.
- Approach gate at 90 km/h (56 mph): faster reads as prey, slower gets told
  to come back at 200 MPH. The zone watches the last 44 m of your run-up.
- Catch gate at 36 km/h (22 mph) after at least 0.8 s in the web; stay
  faster for 12 s, or leave the stretch pocket, and you got away.
- Spider show timings: 6.2 m descent in 2.6 s, 5 s bobbing inspection, 2 s
  retreat back to the crossbeam.
- Breakable web: outer strands snap under the hardest hits, sector by
  sector, and stay snapped until the web re-spins.
- Self re-arming: once the spider retreats and the area stays clear for
  4 s, the prop resets its physics and every snapped strand re-spins —
  "Good as new."

### How to use

Spawn it from the vehicle selector like any prop, then drive a second vehicle
into the web from the marked run-up side — the silk is built to stretch away
from your approach. There are no buttons and no console; the only control is
your speed, and the name is the target. Give it a long flat straight (gridmap
and the airfields are ideal), aim dead centre, and clear the area afterwards
so the web can re-spin.

Built with the shared giant-props framework. Feedback and bug reports welcome.

Fly fast. The web prefers it.
