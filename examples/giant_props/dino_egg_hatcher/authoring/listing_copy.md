## Description

**SOMETHING IS SLEEPING IN THERE** — a speckled egg 6.2 m wide and 7.7 m at
the crown, sitting in a straw nest, cracked open just wide enough to drive
through.

Roll up and the nest tells you exactly what to do: drive in through the
crack. Park inside and the whole shell starts to rattle around you — dust
pouring off the crown, every shard shivering and hopping in place. Hold
still for 2.5 seconds. Then: "RAWR! IT HATCHED!" Seventeen curved shell
fragments burst outward in tumbling ballistic arcs and your car launches
skyward like a newborn dino — 30 m/s straight up (108 km/h / 67 mph) with a
6 m/s shove out over the nest.

While you're airborne, the show runs on schedule: the shards fly 8 m out in
1.1 seconds, bed into the turf over the next 1.4, and the egg sits hatched —
stripped down to its jagged cup — for a full 9 seconds so you can admire
your birth site from altitude. Then the cartoon rewind: a 1.2-second reverse
flight sucks every fragment back into place, seams sealed, egg re-armed,
roughly 13 seconds after it popped.

### The wobble

The pause before the chaos is the whole joke, so the egg commits to it. For
2.5 seconds all seventeen shards rattle at once — 5 cm shivers, 7 cm hops —
while dust erupts from the crown and both flanks. Lose your nerve and back
out through the crack and the show cancels on the spot: "The egg went back
to sleep." No hatch, no launch, no hard feelings. It only hatches if you
stay.

### The shell

These are not chunky breakaway boxes. The seventeen fragments are genuine
curved pieces cut from the dome — seven ring wedges, three crown caps, and
seven wall-band pieces with seams staggered so the cracks brick-lace instead
of lining up. The three wall pieces spanning the doorway carry the arch cut
with them, so the entrance stays open at rest. On hatch, everything above
the 2.3 m rim flies, each shard tumbling at its own rate — the crown caps
drift off lazily while the wall band kicks out flatter — and what stays
standing is a low cracked cup with a jagged rim and the doorway notch, solid
and collidable, like a real broken egg.

### Details

- Full cycle on a fixed clock: 2.5 s wobble, launch, 1.1 s shard burst,
  1.4 s sink, 9 s open, 1.2 s rewind — then it is armed and ready to hatch
  again.
- The launch replaces your velocity cleanly: 30 m/s vertical plus 6 m/s
  forward, aligned to the egg itself — place the prop level and the throw
  goes straight up.
- Abort-aware: leaving mid-wobble (or losing the vehicle entirely) powers
  the egg back down to idle. A wreck never jams the machine.
- A 9 m nest approach zone greets you on the way in: "Something is sleeping
  in there... drive in through the crack!"
- Three dust emitters — crown, left flank, right flank — run from the first
  rattle through the burst.
- Speckled shell in green and teal over eggshell white; two-tone straw nest.
- Books at $26,000 in the vehicle selector. A bargain for a working dinosaur
  egg.

### How to use

Spawn it from the vehicle selector like any prop, then drive a second
vehicle in through the crack — the doorway is the only way in, and the hatch
only fires with a vehicle inside. Give it flat open ground and plenty of
sky; Gridmap works well. There are no buttons and no console: the egg is the
button. Park inside, hold still, and wait.
