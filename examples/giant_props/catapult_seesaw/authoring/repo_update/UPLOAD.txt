WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGED
---------------------

The previous release had a permanently fixed rest-angle collision surface,
collisionless animated plank/weight meshes, and Lua that replaced the subject
vehicle's cluster velocity. This build removes that path completely.

The release now contains:

  * a dynamic 22 x 4.4 m plank box truss with 100 contact nodes;
  * 13.5 m and 8.5 m lever arms for roughly 1.5:1 car-side speed advantage;
  * a reinforced five-node collinear hinge tied into a fixed base;
  * a physical 9,070 kg (10 US tons / 20,000 lb), 75-node counterweight cage
    with a five-node, full-width striker;
  * two breakable weight-latch beams plus 15 zero-preload transport latches,
    all released together before the mechanism moves;
  * a fixed vertical guide rail that aligns, but does not drive, the weight;
  * a 6.0 m gravity-driven free fall into five bounded hydraulic links acting
    through a visible 25 cm rubber mat and five 80 kg structural rib nodes;
  * collidable plank/weight surfaces and weight-base self-collision;
  * node-bound flexbody visuals for the moving plank and weight;
  * a cache-busted ericrolph_catapult_seesaw_physics_v3.dae source path;
  * five final-stroke hydraulic catchers, five one-way rebound snubbers, and
    rubber fail-safe pads; and
  * runtime code limited to release, observation, UI/effects, and safe reset.

The obsolete collisionless plank.dae and weight.dae runtime parts, the old
visual-only ericrolph_catapult_seesaw.dae path, and obsolete physics_v2 shape
are not in the archive. The catapult runtime contains no launchSubject,
addSubjectVelocity, teleportSubject, applyClusterVelocityScaleAdd, or subject
setPositionRotation call.


VALIDATION REQUIRED BEFORE UPLOAD
---------------------------------

Do not paste the serial-27 Pigeon figures here; they describe the obsolete
40-ton/v2 mechanism. The certified serial-78 v3 archive produced these
deterministic Small Grid results:

  * 1,530 kg Barstow I6: 10.18 m/s at 51.5 degrees, +8.00 m apex,
    19.15 m to first ground contact;
  * 1,870 kg Bastion V6: 8.37 m/s at 54.0 degrees, +7.91 m apex,
    15.24 m to first ground contact;
  * block impact 10.71-10.79 m/s, first rebound below 0.96 m, and steady
    parked-plank motion below 0.014 degrees; and
  * no runtime error, NaN, physics-instability, or scripted-launch event.

The required live contract must observe weight_released,
counterweight_impact, physical plank rotation, and seesaw_flung in order. It
must observe no subject_launched event, report no mod errors, and find no
non-finite/NaN/physics-instability entry after the run's log marker.

The input-energy sanity check is a 9,070 kg weight falling 6.0 m: about
10.8 m/s and 534 kJ at impact. Do not advertise the earlier 16-22 m/s or
25 m apex estimates. Those require a strongly elastic collision and conflict
with the deliberately damped, sub-1 m rebound mechanism. Advertise the
measured vehicle-specific results above and label them as reference runs.


NOTE ABOUT FIRST LOAD
---------------------

This archive ships the source PNG textures. BeamNG cooks them into its cache
on first load, so an IMPORTING TEXTURE placeholder may appear briefly the
first time the updated prop is spawned. This is expected and clears once the
one-time texture cook finishes.


LOCAL SIDELOAD TESTING
----------------------

Do not drop this ZIP into a running game while the subscribed release remains
mounted. Fully exit BeamNG first, disable or move the old
mods/repo/catapult_seesaw_ericrolph.zip outside mods, then place this ZIP in
mods and restart. The v3 Collada filename prevents reuse of both the old main
shape cache and the earlier v2 physical cache; a full cache clear is not
required.
