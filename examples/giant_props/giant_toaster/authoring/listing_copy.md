## Description

**THE GIANT TOASTER** — a five-metre chrome two-slice with art-deco end domes
and a bakelite plinth, and it thinks your car is bread.

Bump the browning dial on the base as you drive past — each pass clicks the
power up a level, 1 through 6 — then take the seventeen-metre trestle ramp to
the rooftop landing and roll down into a slot, settling between the glowing
element walls like bread. Nothing happens until you are all the way in. Then:
"Bread detected. Toasting at level 3..." The side lever rides 1.9 m down its
track in half a second, the launch plate dips ten centimetres under you, the
dial needle starts to tremble, and the machine counts — tick... 3, tick... 2,
tick... 1 — 2.8 seconds that take considerably longer than 2.8 seconds. POP.
The plate snaps up 1.6 m in a tenth of a second, steam bursts from the crown,
and your car leaves straight up at whatever speed the dial promises, with a
3 m/s forward nudge so you come down clear of the rim. Leave mid-countdown and
the machine takes it personally: "The toast escaped!"

### The browning dial

The cream dial on the flank of the base is 1.9 m across, with a red pointer
sweeping 150 degrees over six positions. It is a drive-by control: pass within
a couple of metres of it and the level clicks up one — "Browning dial: level 4
of 6" — wrapping from 6 back to 1, with a 0.9-second cooldown so one pass is
one click. The six levels are real launch speeds, and the ladder is steep:

- Level 1 — 12 m/s straight up (43 km/h / 27 mph): a polite hop.
- Level 3 — 23 m/s (83 km/h): the factory default.
- Level 6 — 48 m/s (173 km/h / 107 mph): no longer a kitchen appliance.

The full ladder: 12 / 17 / 23 / 30 / 38 / 48 m/s.

### Two slots, no waiting

It is a real two-slice: both slots run the whole show independently —
separate launch plates, separate countdowns, separate steam vents at the
crown. Toast two cars at once. Each slot is 2.9 m wide and 0.65 m deep behind
a 14-degree entry lip — re-profiled after play-testing so low cars roll in
clean instead of beaching — and each re-arms 3.3 seconds after its pop.

### Details

- Full cycle per slot: arm on full containment, lever drop, plate dip, a
  2.8-second tick-3-2-1 countdown, POP, steam, plate return, re-armed 3.3
  seconds after launch.
- Six dial-set vertical launch speeds from 12 to 48 m/s (43 to 173 km/h),
  plus a 3 m/s forward kick over the rim.
- The launch plate throws 1.6 m upward in a tenth of a second; the lever
  drops 1.9 m down its side track while the toast is in and pops back up
  with it.
- Two fully independent slots — two cars can toast at the same time.
- Escape clause: exit mid-countdown and the cycle aborts with "The toast
  escaped!"
- Chrome shell with art-deco end domes on a bakelite plinth; glowing orange
  heating-element strips line the slot walls.
- Lists at $25,000 in the vehicle selector, which is honestly a fair price
  for a toaster this size.

### How to use

Spawn it from the vehicle selector like any prop, then drive a second vehicle
in. Brush past the dial on the side of the base to set power, climb the
trestle ramp to the rooftop landing, and settle fully into a slot — the
countdown only starts once the whole car is inside. Give it open ground: the
trestle runs about twenty metres out the front, and on level 6 the landing is
a separate adventure. Gridmap and the airfields have the sky for it.
