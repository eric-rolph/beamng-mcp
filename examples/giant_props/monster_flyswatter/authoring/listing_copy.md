## Description

**MONSTER FLYSWATTER** — a 7.2 m swatter arm on a low steel hinge beside a
painted swat lane. Your vehicle is the bug.

Drive into the 6.4 m wide, 20 m long lane and cross the trip band: "bzzzz..."
flashes up and the head — parked at 72 degrees, more than eight metres
overhead — starts to tremble. You get exactly half a second of that. Then the
slam: 78 degrees of arc in 0.22 seconds, accelerating the whole way down,
dust bursting off the asphalt on impact. Anything under the 6.4 × 6.2 m head
at that instant — or bold enough to roll under during the 0.3 s swat window
that follows — leaves at 144 km/h (about 90 mph). SWAT! Then, deliberately,
luxuriously, the machine peels itself off the deck: a 0.8 s hold, a slow
2.2 s rise, a one-second cooldown. And if you are still on the trip band —
or under the head — when it re-arms, it winds up and does it again.

### The timing game

The machine gives you three honest outs:

- **Hug the line.** The head is exactly as wide as the lane, but it lands
  0.7 m off-centre — on purpose. The outer edge is survivable. Put your
  wheels on the paint and hope your sheet metal is narrow.
- **Outrun it.** Tripwire to impact is 0.72 seconds — half a second of
  tremble, 0.22 of slam. Carry enough speed and the head hits your dust.
- **Bait it.** Once the 0.3 s swat window shuts, nothing can touch you for
  3.7 seconds while it holds, rises, and resets. Let it slam on empty
  asphalt, then run.

Squeak out from under the head mid-hold and the machine concedes it on
screen: "Near miss!"

### The flick

When it connects, it connects like a flyswatter. Your velocity is replaced
outright — however fast you came in, you leave at 40 m/s — angled about 27
degrees upward and sideways over the far edge of the lane, with a kick
toward whichever end of the head caught you. Every vehicle under the mesh
gets its own launch, exactly one per slam, and the tally resets every cycle:
campers get swatted again. And again.

### Details

- Full sequence: 0.5 s tremble, 0.22 s slam through 78 degrees, 0.8 s hold,
  2.2 s smooth rise, 1.0 s cooldown — about 4.7 seconds trip to re-armed.
- The tremble is a two-degree quiver at about 7 Hz, with the "bzzzz..."
  warning flashing up the moment you arm it.
- The kill zone is the head's exact 6.4 × 6.2 m footprint. No invisible
  overreach; no mercy inside it.
- The swat window runs 0.3 s past impact, so diving under a landed head
  still counts as contact.
- "Near miss!" is detected and announced when you escape the kill zone
  un-swatted during the hold or rise.
- The slam kicks up a dust burst on impact; it clears when the head lifts.
- Camp under the swatter and the cooldown re-arms it automatically — you
  stay on the menu until you leave.
- Worn red plastic frame and handle, woven green mesh, steel hinge on a
  hazard-striped 1.5 m pedestal, and a painted lane with edge lines and
  centre dashes.

### How to use

Spawn it from the vehicle selector like any prop, then drive a second
vehicle in. It wants a flat stretch of open ground about 20 m long. The lane
slab sits just 3 cm proud of the terrain, so there is no ramp to climb —
drive straight on. The trip band lies across the approach end of the lane;
the swatter wakes the moment you cross it. Gridmap and the airfields work
well.
