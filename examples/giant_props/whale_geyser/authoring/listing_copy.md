## Description

**THE TAIL IS THE RAMP** — a 33 m fin whale, beached, friendly, and fully
drivable, with a blowhole that fires cars instead of mist.

Drive up the steel trestle from behind the tail — a 23 m run, 4.6 m wide,
passing under the raised fluke and landing where the back widens. The whale
greets you: "You are driving on a whale. It doesn't mind." Follow the spine
to the head and park fully on the blowhole pad, 4.3 m above the ground.

Then the bit: mist wisps curl out of the blowhole, the screen reads "The
whale is inhaling...", and for 1.5 seconds nothing else happens. Hold still.

THAR SHE BLOWS. A five-stage water column erupts from the pad to 22 m up, and
the whale sets your speed outright — 55 m/s straight up, which is 198 km/h
(123 mph), plus a 3 m/s forward push — for an apex the spec sheet calls
~150 m: about 500 feet. It vents spray for 3 seconds, settles for 4, and is
re-armed 7 seconds after the blast. The landing is your problem.

### The inhale

The pack's design rule is "exaggerate the anticipation", and this prop is
almost nothing else:

- The pad is a strict fully-contained trigger — half-on doesn't count — but
  its 6.6 × 6.8 m detection volume was enlarged after play-testing so an
  honestly driven, slightly crooked park still registers.
- Roll off mid-inhale and you get: "The whale exhales, disappointed."
- Loiter on the back without parking and every 7 seconds it reminds you:
  "Park fully ON the blowhole pad at the head!"
- At idle the blowhole puffs a small wisp every 8 seconds. It's breathing.
  It's fine.

### The tail ramp

The hero whale's tail is a raised catwalk with a fluke notch, so the drive-on
route is a research trestle at a maximum grade of about 17 degrees —
re-graded after play-testing said the original was too steep. It lands onto
16.5 m of drivable spine that tapers from 4.4 m wide at the landing to 2.4 m
near the head. The collision deck is ray-sampled from the hero mesh at 12
stations, so the back you see is the back you drive, squashed dorsal bump
included.

### Details

- Full cycle: park → 1.5 s inhale → blast → 3 s venting → 4 s settle,
  re-armed 7 seconds after launch.
- The blast replaces your velocity rather than adding to it: 55 m/s up plus
  3 m/s forward, no matter how fast you arrived.
- Five stacked water emitters stage the column from pad height to 22 m; a
  sixth handles the idle mist puffs.
- Land back on the pad once it has re-armed and the whale simply inhales
  again.
- Realistic fin-whale hero body flattened into a beached stance, dorsal fin
  pre-squashed into a drivable bump.
- Running commentary from "You are driving on a whale. It doesn't mind."
  through "THAR SHE BLOWS!"
- Listed at $27,000 in the vehicle selector, which is a bargain for a whale.

### How to use

Spawn it from the vehicle selector like any prop, then drive a second vehicle
in — the approach is from behind the tail, up the trestle ramp. There are no
buttons: the whole control scheme is where you park. Give it flat open ground
(a 33 m whale plus a 23 m ramp) and, unusually for a prop, clear sky — the
ride tops out around 150 m. Gridmap and the airfields work well.
