## Description

**VERTICAL VEHICLE PACHINKO TOWER** — a pachinko board stood on end, 24 m wide
under a 52 m mast, that plays your car as the ball.

Drive onto the carriage deck at the base of the lift shaft — exactly where a
real pachinko machine feeds its balls — and hold still. The tower counts you
down, calls GOING UP!, and the chain hoist carries you 43 m up the mast while
the counterweight sinks past you on the other side of the loop. That climb
takes thirteen and a half seconds. Use them to make peace with the board below.

At the crown the release gate swings open and the deck tips in ratcheted
0.8-second clunks — 17, then 33, then 50 degrees, past the friction angle of
locked rubber on steel, so braking does not save you. Down the 40-degree chute
you go, into the pegs, ricocheting toward one of five scoring bins. The machine
reads where you actually came to rest, puffs dust in that bin, and pays out —
from 400 points to the 10,000-point JACKPOT.

### The board

Twenty-five kite-section steel pegs — 2.20 m across, on a surface with
literally zero friction — in eight staggered rows, and the geometry is the
game: the staggered rows leave a widest lane of 1.60 m against a 2.0 m car, so
there is no clean line through the board — proven row by row at build time, so
there is no lucky lane anywhere. Between rows you get 1.91 m of air to tumble
in, and a 5.40 m throat between neighbours that no car can wedge across at any
angle. The bins
pay 3000 / 1500 / 10,000 / 800 / 400 left to right; you enter at the top
right, so the farther the pegs knock you, the more it's worth — except the one
bin that beats them all. The jackpot is hard by geometry, not dice: leaning
horn dividers choke the middle bin's mouth to 2.60 m, so a car only drops in
near-vertical or dead aligned. Land in a bin and you stay landed — the pockets
are 4.65 m deep, taller than a car plus its bounce. Miss everything and you
roll out the gutter: no score, back to the loader.

### The scoreboard is a machine

No screens, no fake glow — the readout is a pointer trolley on a rail across
the bin fascia. It drives to the centre of the bin you actually landed in,
crossing the full 19.2 m fascia in 2.1 seconds, and holds there while your
score is up. Score nothing and it parks past the end of the scale, which is
its own kind of message. While the tower waits for a player it sweeps the
fascia as an attract mode, one full round trip every 10.1 seconds.

### Details

- Fully automatic cycle: hold still on the deck (2.5 s below 0.7 m/s) and the
  tower does the rest — hoist at 3.2 m/s, gate, three-detent tip, the fall,
  the payout, then a self-reload with the carriage coming home at 5.2 m/s.
- Escalation for clingers: the 50-degree final detent beats locked-tyre
  friction (~45 degrees); hold on anyway and the deck raps you with a TILT!,
  then dumps to 68 degrees — holding that would take two and a half times the
  friction rubber actually has.
- A car wedged across two pegs gets the cabinet knocker: a 4.5 m car can
  bridge pegs 3.4 m apart, so the board raps it loose with a 25 cm hop and a
  sideways shove, at most once every 1.6 s.
- Scoring is read from the car's real resting position — no scripted outcome —
  and a dust burst fires in the winning bin.
- Loose-car rule: a car that reaches the board without riding the hoist
  (knocked in by another prop, or driven off the chute lip) is adopted and
  played as it lies.
- Crown interlock: stop on the docked deck and the carriage refuses to come
  down with you aboard — it holds for 12 seconds, asks you to drive off, then
  goes home regardless, so a wreck never bricks the tower.
- Cream-and-red board, dark gunmetal pegs, and marquee signage: PACHINKO TOWER
  on the crown, DRIVE IN - HOLD STILL at the loader, and a value plate over
  every bin, including the red JACKPOT 10000.
- Real machinery throughout: a closed chain loop over turning sheaves, a
  counterweight riding opposite the carriage, and a 4.4% drive-out apron from
  the bin floor back to grade.

### How to use

Spawn it from the vehicle selector like any prop, then drive a second vehicle
in. Take the ramp to the lift shaft, drive fully onto the carriage deck —
4.8 × 6.6 m, more than a metre of margin around a compact car — and hold
still. After the payout, drive forward out of the bins and the tower reloads
for the next player. It is a 24 m board under a 52 m mast plus the lift shaft
and ramps, so give it open, flat ground. Gridmap and the airfields work well.

Hold still. The rest is gravity.
